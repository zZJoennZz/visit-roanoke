What to Expect

Celebrate Independence Day with an evening of free, family-friendly fun in the heart of Downtown Roanoke.

Spectacular Fireworks

A breathtaking fireworks show lights up the sky just after 9:15 PM over downtown Roanoke.

Live Entertainment

Dueling piano performance at City Hall Plaza starting at 7:30 PM, high-energy & interactive.

Carnival & Games

Carnival rides, bounce houses, face painting, and a pie-eating competition for all ages.

Food Trucks

Delicious food trucks serving BBQ, tacos, sweet treats, and cold drinks all evening.

Vendor Village

Browse local vendor booths selling handmade goods, art, jewelry, and more along Oak Street.

Patriotic Celebration

Honor Independence Day and 250 years of America with your family and community.
