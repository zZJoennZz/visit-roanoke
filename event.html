Questions?

Everything you need to know before you go.

Is there an admission fee?

No — Taste & Tunes is free and open to the public. Food, drinks, and vendor items are available for purchase.

Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved dogs on leashes are welcome in the outdoor festival area. Please clean up after your pet.

Can I bring outside food or drinks?

Outside alcohol is not permitted. Sealed water bottles and small snacks are fine. We encourage supporting our food trucks!

What if it rains?

Taste & Tunes is a rain-or-shine event. In case of severe weather, updates will be posted on our social media and website.

Still have questions? Reach out to the events team.
